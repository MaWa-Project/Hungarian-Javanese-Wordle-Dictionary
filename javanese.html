Game - Javanese

Guess the 5-letter Javanese word in 6 tries:

Ignored Absent
Correct Correct (wrong diacritic)
Present Present (wrong diacritic)

Enter your guesses using the keyboard. Use Backspace to delete. The game will suggest possible words based on your inputs.

Note: Javanese words contain diacritical marks. Making a guess with the wrong diacritics will still count as correct/present, but will be indicated with a different color.

For additional information, see the page about the Javanese Language.
